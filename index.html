FitBowl

Smart food portions, weight tracking and daily care for dogs and cats.

FitBowl is an offline-first pet food calculator and care tracker. Calculate practical daily food portions, follow your pet's weight and keep everyday care in one simple place.

What FitBowl can do

  • Calculate daily food portions for dogs and cats
  • Track weight and body condition
  • Save different foods and calorie densities
  • Track meals, treats, water and activity
  • Create medication, vaccination and care reminders
  • Manage multiple pets
  • Export reports as CSV or PDF

Private by default

FitBowl does not require an account or FitBowl cloud synchronization. Pet information is primarily stored locally on your Android device.
